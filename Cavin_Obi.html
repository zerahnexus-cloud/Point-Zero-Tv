Cavin Obi

AFROSWING • DANCEHALL • AFROBEAT • REGGAE

Cavin Obi is a rising Kenyan artist blending Afroswing, Dancehall, Afrobeat, and Reggae into a unique sound that connects with both African and international audiences. Known for his energetic delivery, street-inspired storytelling, and vibrant melodies, Cavin Obi continues to build a strong identity in the new generation of African music.

With a passion for creating music that inspires, entertains, and represents the culture, Cavin Obi is focused on growing his fanbase across Africa and beyond through authentic sound, strong visuals, and live performances.
